Work About Resume

Hi there!

You probably already know my name by now, but I'm Livianne! I'm born and raised in Los Angeles, CA and just recently graudated from UC San Diego.

My perspective on design started at home. Growing up, I watched my older family members struggle with technology, not because they couldn't use it, but because so many products weren't designed with them in mind. It made me realize how easily confusing design can become a barrier and sparked my interest in accessibility and information architecture.

I design by understanding how people experience technology and letting those experiences shape what I create. Technology can open up endless possibilities, but those possibilities mean little without the people who experience them. I hope the things I design can find a place and a home somewhere in the world.

Learning by Doing

I started my journey in community college with nothing but a computer and a dream. At the time, I barely knew how to zoom in on Figma, but through curiosity, YouTube tutorials, and countless TikToks, I slowly taught myself design and prototyping from the ground up.

I still remember making an absolutely ridiculous burger in Figma and feeling like a creative genius. I didn't fully understand product design yet, but I knew I loved the building, learning, and improving aspects of it. That curiosity is still a big part of how I approach design today.

Outside the File

When I'm not building projects, you'll usually find me cooking, baking, or experimenting in the kitchen! My love for food comes from the joy of bringing people together - something that also inspires the way I approach design and creativity. Whether through design, food, or music, I'm always exploring new ideas and turning them into meaningful, memorable experiences.
